Privacy Policy — Pic and color

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Pic and color (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a colouring app for young children, published by Aniket Pralhad Ambadkar]. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. It is written to be short and specific, because the honest answer is that the app collects nothing.

Summary. Pic and color does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information about anyone, including children.

The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. It does not have permission to access the internet, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere. Everything the child makes stays on the device.

We do not collect any data

We collect no personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact details
  • User accounts, passwords, or login credentials of any kind
  • Photographs or images
  • Location data of any kind, precise or approximate
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID), or persistent IDs
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or SMS data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, behavioural data, or diagnostics
  • Any information from or about children

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal information to third parties, because we do not have any.

The app has no internet access

Pic and color does not request the Android INTERNET permission and contains no networking code. Android will not permit the app to open a network connection. This is not merely a policy commitment — it is enforced by the operating system. No information can be transmitted from the app to us or to anyone else.

We, the developers, have no servers, no database, and no ability to see anything a child creates or does in the app.

Information stored on your own device

The app saves the following only in its own private storage on your device. This information is not accessible to us, to other apps, or to anyone else, and it never leaves the device:

WhatWhy
Colouring progress for each pictureSo a child can return to an unfinished picture
Colouring pages created from photos (line art)So they appear in the child’s gallery
Small thumbnail previewsTo show the gallery grid
A sound on/off preferenceTo remember the setting
A count of how many pictures have been createdTo apply the app’s usage limit

You can permanently erase all of it at any time by uninstalling the app, or via Settings → Apps → Pic and color → Storage → Clear data on your device.

How the camera is used

Pic and color can turn a photo into a colouring page. This feature is placed behind a parental gate — a simple arithmetic question intended to be answered by an adult, not a young child.

When a photo is taken:

  1. The photo is held in the device’s memory only. It is never written to storage.
  2. It is converted into black-and-white line art entirely on the device, by code inside the app. No external service, cloud provider, or AI service is involved.
  3. The original photograph is discarded from memory immediately once the line art is created, or as soon as the user retakes the photo or leaves the camera screen.
  4. Only the resulting line-art drawing is saved.

The original photograph is never saved to disk, never added to your photo gallery by the app, never backed up, and never transmitted. It cannot be recovered after the camera screen is closed.

We consider this the most important privacy property of the app: a photograph of a child is used for a few seconds in memory and then destroyed.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Camera — Required only to take a photo to convert into a colouring page. Used solely for the purpose described above. The app never opens the camera without the user choosing to, and the feature sits behind the parental gate. You may decline this permission; the rest of the app works normally without it.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — Required only to save a finished picture into your device’s photo gallery, and only when a user explicitly chooses to save. On Android 10 and newer, no storage permission is requested at all.

The app requests no other permissions.

Saving and sharing pictures

A user may choose to save a finished picture to the device’s photo gallery, or to share it using the standard Android share sheet (for example, to a messaging or email app).

These actions only ever happen when a user deliberately chooses them — nothing is saved outside the app or shared automatically. Once a picture is shared into another app, that picture is handled by that other app and is governed by that app’s own privacy policy, not this one. We recommend that a parent or guardian supervises the sharing of any picture.

Children’s privacy (COPPA and GDPR-K)

This app is intended for use by young children and is designed to comply with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including provisions concerning children, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and Google Play’s Families policies.

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, there is no children’s data for us to store, use, disclose, or delete. Consequently:

  • We do not require verifiable parental consent, because we collect nothing that would require it.
  • There is no personal data of a child for a parent to request access to, correct, or delete.
  • We do not condition a child’s use of the app on disclosing more information than is necessary.
  • We do not use children’s data for advertising, profiling, or any form of targeting.

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics

The app contains no advertisements of any kind, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or measurement SDKs, no social network SDKs, and no third-party tracking technologies. We do not build profiles of users and do not use the Android Advertising ID.

In-app purchases

The current version of the app contains no in-app purchases and no payments. If purchasing is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any purchase flow will be placed behind a parental gate as required by Google Play’s Families policies.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Information stored locally on your device stays there until you remove it. To erase everything the app has stored, uninstall the app or clear its data from your device settings.

Pictures a user has explicitly saved to the device photo gallery, or shared to another app, are your own files and remain where you put them; they are not removed by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised “Last updated” date. If a future version of the app ever begins collecting data — which we have no plan to do — we will update this policy and seek any consent required by law before that version is released.
